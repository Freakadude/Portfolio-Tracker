# ADR 0029: News triage by the LLM

Status: accepted (2026-10-05)

## Decisions
- **One call per batch, linking and scoring together.** The spec describes a Haiku classification for thematic and macro links and a Haiku assessment as separate steps. Folio does both in one structured call per batch of up to eight stories, because the model reads the same text for both and the two calls would double the input cost against a 1.50 EUR news share of a 5 EUR month. A batch call and its answer are one `agent_run` of type `news_assess` with the full prompt and answer kept for the trace.
- **The model only picks from what it is given.** The user message lists the holdings by weight (never an amount) and the sleeves the strategy watches, each with a ref (`instrument:12`, `sleeve:gold_hedge`), then each story: what is already linked, and its headlines and summaries inside an `<untrusted>` block. The JSON schema pins `cluster_id`, `affected` and link `target` to enums of exactly those refs, so the model cannot name a story or holding it was not shown; the answer is checked again in code (an unknown value is dropped and reported), enum values are compared without regard to case, and the score is clamped to 0 to 100 by us because structured outputs carry no numeric bounds.
- **Outside text is data.** Anything from a feed is stripped of angle brackets before it is placed in the prompt, so a headline cannot close the `<untrusted>` block or open a new one; the system prompt says what to do with instructions found in it (ignore them, score a story that tries to give instructions as a story about that attempt); and the model has no tool that could act on them. A test feeds a headline that tries to close the block and a model that was fooled, and checks that nothing from it reaches an assessment or a link.
- **Who is read.** Stories linked to a holding with a relevance of at least 0.05, newest and most relevant first, from the last three days. Stories linked to nothing are also read, but only from sources the owner trusts (0.5 or more) and at most ten a day, since a thematic story ("export rules on chip-making equipment") is exactly what text matching cannot link. These limits, the batch size and the thresholds are the `news` settings section.
- **Escalation.** A story scoring 60 or more, or touching a position above 10 % of the portfolio, is assessed again alone with the stronger model and more of its text; the new assessment is stored beside the first and the later one is shown. Impact of 80 or more on a holding becomes one high notification per story, with a lock-screen text that names no holding; the agent's event run (step 8) reads stories at or above impact 70.
- **Feedback adjusts, in small steps.** "Not relevant" marks lower a source's trust by 0.1 for every five more "not relevant" than "useful" marks (between 0.1 and 1), and "useful" ones raise it back; a mark on a wrongly linked story also lowers the weight of the alias that found it by 0.2 (it stops matching below 0.3), and "useful" restores 0.1. Marking a story the same way twice counts once; marking it the other way undoes the first mark on the source.
- **Triage runs inside the news job**, after fetching and linking, and only when the agent is on and has a key. A refused key or an account without credit fails the run (the owner has to act); a spent budget, a rate limit or an unreadable answer is logged and the stories wait for the next run, which is every 15 minutes.

## Consequences
Triage costs a few cents a day for a handful of linked stories, and is bounded by the news share of the budget. A thematic link is only as good as the model's reading of two sentences; each carries `llm:` in `matched_by` so it can be told from a text match and is never replaced when the text matches are redone.
