# ADR 0023: The notification pipeline: inbox first, then a dispatcher

Status: accepted (2026-10-05)

## Decisions
- **The inbox is the source of truth.** Every signal of the active strategy, every price alert, system notice and digest is first stored as a `notification`. Pushes are separate `notification_delivery` rows, one per phone channel, so the inbox never depends on a phone channel working. Creating an item publishes a `notification` event on the existing server-sent-event stream, so the unread count in the browser updates without a reload. Shadow strategies' signals never become notifications.
- **Routing is decided when the item is created**, from the severity × channel matrix in Settings, Notifications (defaults from spec section 13: critical, high and medium to the phone, low and info to the inbox and the digest only; the digest itself is pushed). So a changed cell applies to the next item, not to ones already queued. A channel that is routed but not set up is logged as skipped, with what to fill in. The wizard's channel choice "none" switches pushes off entirely.
- **A dispatcher in the worker, every minute**, sends what is due. Critical goes out at once, through quiet hours and past the cap. Otherwise, in quiet hours (default 22:00 to 07:30, in the owner's timezone) everything is held until they end; medium items wait for the next full hour, so they arrive together; items on the same subject that arrive together, or within an hour of the last push on that subject, are merged into one push ("3 updates on equity"); and past the daily cap (default 5, critical not counted) the rest wait for the next morning. Nothing is dropped: held items are sent in the next allowed window.
- **Lock-screen text is written, not filtered.** Each item carries its full inbox text and two push texts made by the code that created it: one without euro amounts (the default) and one without amounts or names (the private mode). Push titles are generic ("Folio: important"); only digests have their own. A tap opens the item in Folio when the app address is set.
- **Retries and the delivery log.** A failed send is retried after one and after five minutes; after the third attempt the delivery is marked failed and keeps the channel's own error ("Home Assistant refused the token (HTTP 401)"). A merged push is retried as one. Each inbox item lists its deliveries.
- **Channels:** the Home Assistant companion app through Home Assistant's REST notify service, and ntfy, published as JSON so titles with accents survive. Each has a "send test" that uses the saved settings and shows the error if any. **Web Push is deferred** to Phase 5: it needs HTTPS, VAPID keys and a service worker and was not among the owner's choices (Q5). The channel interface leaves room for it.
- **Digests are deterministic** for now: the value and the result over the day (or the week on Sundays), the open items by severity, and what is coming up (contribution, thesis reviews). They are written once a day after the configured time (default 18:30) by the same minute job, so a changed time applies the same day. The agent's daily review (Phase 4) will add to them.
- **The minute job writes no job-run row**, which would bury the System page; deliveries have their own log, and failures of the job itself go to the log like any other job error.

## Consequences
A push can arrive up to a minute after the item is created. If the worker is down, items still reach the inbox (the web process creates none, but the rules job and alerts run in the worker too), and pushes catch up when it returns, held items included.
