# ADR 0061: News stories as separable cards that say what they touch and what could happen

Status: accepted (2026-10-10). Extends FR-NW-05, FR-NW-06 and FR-NW-07; new scope, asked for by the owner.

## Context
A story on the News page was one bordered box of small text: the title, a row of badges, the assessment, the links and the sources all at the same weight. Titles, summary, impact and "what does this touch" could not be told apart, nor one story from the next. Stories whose assessment said they affect nothing of the owner's were still listed. And the assessment only said how big the effect is now, not what it could become, nor what to watch.

## Decision
- **A card in bands.** Each story is a card with a coloured stripe (by the potential of the effect), a large title, and separate bands: *What it touches*, *What was reported*, *Impact now*, *What could happen*, *What you could do*, and a footer with the feedback buttons. Direction is a word with an icon, never colour alone.
- **How the story reaches the owner is told by wording and style.** Four groups, each with its own heading and chip style: *Hits your holding directly* (solid chip, with the holding's share of the portfolio), *Reaches you through a fund* (outlined chip: "the fund: company is 5.2 % of the fund"), *Might affect you indirectly* (dashed chip: a theme or macro link the model found, with its reason, and sleeves), and *Touches something you only watch* (watchlist instruments).
- **No effect, no card.** `news_cluster.affects_owner` is set by one pure rule (`news/service.effect_of`): before assessment, any link to a holding, watched instrument or sleeve counts; after it, the assessment must name affected holdings, or the model must have added a link, or a sleeve is reached with a score of 20 or more. Stories without it are not listed and leave no trace on the page (no footnote, no toggle). `GET /news` still has `include_unlinked` for tools and tests; the dashboard news widget and the helper's `get_news` use the same flag.
- **The assessment says what could happen.** Four new fields: `outlook_term` (short: days, mid: weeks to months, long: a year or more), `outlook_level` (low, mid, high: the potential size of the effect, which may differ from today's score), `outlook` (what it could mean, including through the companies inside a fund) and `advice` (only for mid or high). To reason about funds and fit the advice to the owner, the prompt carries each held fund's eight largest holdings with weights and the owner's strategy in short (principles and theses as written, sleeve targets and bands, percentage risk limits, the series each sleeve watches); no euro amounts leave the machine, as before. Prompt `news_assess` is version 2.
- **Advice is guidance, not a recommendation.** It may say which way to lean ("consider trimming X if it passes its threshold") and what to watch, because the owner asked for it. It goes through no gate and creates no draft, order or recommendation; a text that quotes an amount of money or a number of units is dropped by the parser, advice is cut when the potential is low, and the card labels it AI-generated guidance and not an order. Recommendations with orders remain the gated agent flow (ADR 0031).
- **Old assessments.** Until a story is assessed again, its term and level are derived from the stored horizon (intraday and days short, weeks mid, structural long) and score (under 40 low, 40 to 69 mid, 70 and over high), and the card says they are estimated. Migration 0015 puts the stories of the last seven days back to "not assessed" once, so the normal news job assesses them again in its batches under the monthly budget.
- **What was reported stays a summary.** FR-NW-03 still holds: only the headline, the feed's summary (500 characters at most) and the link are stored, never an article body. The card says "Summary from the source" and links to it, so an excerpt is not taken for the whole article.

## Consequences
An assessment call is a little larger (fund holdings, strategy, four more fields per story), so the cheap model's output allowance per story went up; the budget guard is unchanged. A story a rule found by name but the model judged irrelevant disappears from the page, which is the point; "not relevant" feedback still moves source trust and alias weights as before. The migration's re-assessment spends a few cents the first time.
